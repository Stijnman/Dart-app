## Vision
Transform Dart Game Pro into the most complete, intelligent, and engaging darts platform — from casual play to serious competitive training and community features.

---

## Current State (v2.3 – Live)

- 30 game modes with universal engine
- DartBot (12 levels) + partial SmartBot
- Career mode, ELO, rankings, 15 PDC events
- 35 achievements
- Analytics (basic heatmaps + trends)
- Tournaments
- Voice input (basic)
- Clean architecture + 102 tests
- Streamlit UI with 9 tabs

**Progress Update**: Customizable Themes and Voice Commands foundation added.
